About CODEALPHA

CODEALPHA is a demo e-commerce experience built for the CodeAlpha Full Stack Development Internship — Task 1. Our mission is simple: Smart Shopping. Better Choices. We bring together trending electronics, fashion, home essentials and more into one clean, fast, browser-based shopping experience.

This entire storefront — from product browsing to checkout — runs directly in your browser using localStorage, with no backend or server required. It's built to demonstrate a complete, production-quality front-end shopping flow using only vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

📦 Shipping Policy

  • Delivery time: 3–7 business days across India. Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai) typically receive orders in 2–4 business days.
  • Free shipping: Free on all prepaid orders above ₹75 equivalent order value; a flat shipping fee applies below that.
  • Order processing: Orders are processed within 24 hours of confirmation, Monday–Saturday.
  • Tracking: Once shipped, use the "Track Order" option with your Order ID and email to see live status.
  • COD orders: Cash on Delivery orders may take 1–2 extra days for verification before dispatch.
  • Serviceable areas: We currently ship to most pin codes across India. Remote/rural areas may experience longer delivery windows.

↩️ Returns & Refund Policy

  • Return window: 7 days from the date of delivery for most products.
  • Condition: Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags intact.
  • How to initiate: Go to Account → Orders, select the order, and choose "Return Item," or contact support with your Order ID.
  • Refund timeline: Once the returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method (or as store credit for COD orders).
  • Non-returnable items: Personal care/beauty items, innerwear and certain hygiene-sensitive products cannot be returned once opened.
  • Exchanges: Size/color exchanges are available for eligible fashion items within the return window.

🔒 Privacy Policy

  • Data we collect: Name, email, phone, shipping address and order history, only to process and deliver your orders.
  • Local storage: This demo store saves your cart, wishlist, account and orders in your browser's localStorage — it is not transmitted to any external server.
  • Payment data: We never store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled securely through trusted third-party gateways.
  • Cookies: Used only to remember your preferences (theme, cart) for a smoother shopping experience.
  • Third-party sharing: We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
  • Your rights: You may request access, correction or deletion of your data anytime by contacting support@codealpha.in.
